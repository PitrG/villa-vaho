Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

In short: this website does not collect any personal data. It has no contact forms, no analytics, no tracking and no cookies.

What happens when you visit

When you open the website, your browser automatically sends technical information (such as your IP address, date and time of the visit, the page requested and your browser type) to our hosting provider. This is needed to deliver the website and keep it secure. We do not use this information to identify you, and it is not combined with any other data.

Fonts, images and the map are served from our own website. The map is a still image based on data from OpenStreetMap, so no data is sent to any map or font service when you view this website.

When you contact us

If you write to us by email or call us, we use your details only to answer your enquiry and, if you book, to arrange your stay. We keep them only as long as needed for that purpose or as required by law.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device.

Links to other websites

This website links to Google Maps, Booking.com and Airbnb. These links open the other websites only when you click them. Once you are there, their own privacy policies and cookie rules apply.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. To use these rights, contact us at info@villavaho.com.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Croatian data protection authority: Agencija za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.
